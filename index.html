About Us

Ebonmane Ink Creative Studios is a collective of passionate creators dedicated to crafting epic narratives, innovative tools, and engaging experiences. Our mission is to empower storytellers and elevate the art of creation through collaboration and community.

We blend technical expertise with creative vision to bring unique projects to life. From narrative design to software development, our diverse skill set allows us to tackle challenges and deliver quality solutions that resonate with audiences.

At Ebonmane Ink, we believe in the power of storytelling to connect people, inspire change, and foster understanding. We are committed to inclusivity, representation, and creating spaces where all voices can be heard. Join us on this journey as we explore new frontiers in creativity and innovation.

Whether you're a creator looking for collaboration, a gamer seeking deep experiences, or simply someone who appreciates the art of storytelling, we invite you to explore our projects and join our community. Together, we can shape the future of creative expression.

Jordan Campbell (aka Jayden King)

Founder and Lead Developer at Ebonmane Ink Creative Studios. With a passion for narrative design and technical innovation, Jordan leads the team in crafting unique experiences that empower creators and engage audiences.

With a background in software development and a love for storytelling, Jordan combines technical expertise with creative vision to bring projects to life. From building interactive tools to designing powerful narratives, Jordan is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what's possible in the realm of creative technology.

Outside of work, Jordan enjoys exploring new technologies, playing tabletop games, and engaging with the creative community. Always eager to learn and grow, Jordan is committed to fostering an inclusive and collaborative environment at Ebonmane Ink.
